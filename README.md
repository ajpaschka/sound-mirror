# Sound Mirror

Sound Mirror listens to your voice or instrument. It shows how you sound and how you should sound.

Open the page: https://ajpaschka.github.io/sound-mirror/

## How to use it

1. Open the page on a phone or a computer.
2. Choose your voice or instrument, a key, and a scale.
3. Tap **Listen**. Allow the microphone when the browser asks.
4. Sing or play. The page shows your pitch against the notes you should hit.
5. Tap **Guide me**. The page plays each note of the scale. You answer it.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. The page does not record or send audio.

## Languages

English, Spanish, Portuguese, Hindi, Marathi. These match the five most common languages of the account's followers. Use the language menu at the top.
The translations were not checked by a native speaker. Instrument names, scale names, and note names stay in English.
