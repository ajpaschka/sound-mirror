    Help

    1. Choose your voice or instrument, a key and a scale.
    2. Tap Listen and allow the microphone.
    3. Sing or play. Ink is you, blue is where you should be.
    4. Tap Guide me to hear each note and answer it.

    Voice tips

    • Take a fuller breath and keep the air moving to the end of the note. Flat often goes with fading support.
    • Soften the jaw, drop the tongue, loosen the neck. Sharp often goes with squeezing.
    • Let the voice lighten into head voice instead of pushing chest voice higher.
    • Many teachers use a lip trill or a straw hum for a few minutes.
    • If you cannot hear yourself, you cannot correct. Use one earbud or sing closer to the speaker.
